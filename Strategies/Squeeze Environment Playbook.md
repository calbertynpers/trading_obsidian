## Squeeze-and-Collapse Pattern (NEW)

A specific sub-pattern worth trading:

**Setup:** Coin squeezes hard (3-13x) on short liquidations, then collapses immediately back to pre-squeeze levels.

**Examples:** STO (13x → collapse), ORDI (+340%, $2.5B volume → likely topped)

**How to trade:**
1. Wait for the squeeze to exhaust (volume spike → volume collapse, upper wick rejection)
2. Enter short AFTER the collapse begins, not during the squeeze
3. Target: pre-squeeze level
4. Stop: above the squeeze high
5. This is the one FTP short setup that works well in squeeze environments

**Why it works:** The squeeze burns all the fuel (short liquidations). Once liquidations are done, there's no buying pressure left. The collapse is gravity.

**Risk:** If the squeeze is crime-driven (Type 1 from [[Crime Coins]]), it may NOT collapse. Only trade this on Type 2 (squeeze-and-collapse) coins.

## When Does This Regime End?

Squeeze regimes typically end when:
- Funding rates normalize across the board (shorts stop piling in)
- Open interest declines (less fuel for squeezes)
- BTC enters a clear trend (up or down) — squeezes happen most in choppy/transitional markets
- A major risk-off event kills speculative appetite

Until then: flat + opportunistic > passive positioning.
