### 🆕 Fed Reserve Management Purchases — Active Now (Apr 20 Update)

The New York Fed announced **$40.5 billion in Reserve Management Purchases (RMPs)** running from April 14 to May 13, 2026. Liquidity hits the market nearly every week for one month through scheduled Treasury bill purchases.

**Context:**
- The FOMC unanimously agreed to launch RMPs in December 2025 to prevent bank reserves from draining below safe levels during tax season
- This is technically "not QE" — it's reserve management to keep the banking system's plumbing functioning
- However, the practical effect is the same: cash enters the banking system weekly, increasing liquidity available for lending and investment
- This follows a pattern of escalating Fed liquidity operations since late 2025: $13.5B (Dec 1), $74.6B repo (Dec 31), $18.5B (Feb), $8B (Mar), now $40.5B RMP program (Apr-May)
- IMF baseline projects fed funds reaching 3.25-3.50% by end-2026, with economy returning to full employment and 2% inflation by early 2027
- Boston Fed President Collins stated the Fed is "absolutely prepared" to inject liquidity if markets break down

**Why this matters for crypto:**
- Liquidity injections lift risk assets — historically correlated with BTC and altcoin rallies
- The injection window (Apr 14 – May 13) overlaps perfectly with the FOMC meeting (Apr 28-29)
- Combined with QE already running at $40B/month, total liquidity support is substantial
- Small-cap alts are the highest-beta play on expanding liquidity — directly supports bottom-fishing longs (BIO, BLUR, SAGA) and popular coin recovery watchlist
- The "not QE" framing is irrelevant for markets — what matters is the direction of liquidity flow, and it's flowing in

**What to watch:**
- Does the FOMC Apr 28-29 statement acknowledge the RMP program or signal comfort with current liquidity levels?
- Do bank reserves stabilize above the "ample" threshold after tax season drains?
- If RMPs become recurring beyond May 13, that's a structural shift — de facto permanent QE regardless of label
