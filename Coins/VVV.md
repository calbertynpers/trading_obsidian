## Project Overview

Venice.ai is a decentralised AI inference platform — a private, uncensored alternative to ChatGPT/Claude. Users get text, image, and code generation running on open-source models (Llama, Mistral, etc.) with no data logging and no central authority. Venice cannot be censored or restricted by governments or corporations.

**Founded:** May 2024
**Founder:** Erik Voorhees — high-credibility builder; previously founded ShapeShift (major non-custodial crypto exchange). Known for privacy and decentralisation conviction.
**Users:** 450,000+ (April 2026)
**Revenue model:** API access / subscription; portion of revenue used for VVV buyback and burn

### Why It Exists

Claude, ChatGPT, and other centralized AI providers log all conversations, can be censored, and require identity. Venice offers a privacy-preserving alternative — particularly relevant for crypto traders, developers, and sovereign individuals who don't want AI responses shaped by corporate policy.

---

## Token Mechanics

### VVV as an Access Key

Staking VVV grants a **perpetual, pro-rata share of Venice's total API inference capacity**. Stake 1% of all VVV → receive 1% of all Venice API capacity with no per-request fees. This is a real utility token — staking is how you access the network, not just a governance vote.

### DIEM (Dual Token System — launched Aug 2025)

Lock staked VVV to mint **DIEM** tokens. Each DIEM guarantees exactly $1 of daily API credit, permanently. Converts VVV stake into predictable, dollar-denominated compute access.

| Emission dilution | ~14% annually — partially offset by burns |

### Deflationary Mechanisms

1. **Buyback and burn** — launched Nov 2025. A portion of Venice.ai monthly revenue buys VVV from the open market and burns it permanently. Direct link between platform revenue and token scarcity.
2. **Emission reduction cadence** — scheduled step-downs reduce supply growth over time.
3. **Unclaimed airdrop burn** — March 2025: team burned unclaimed airdrop tokens and repurchased sold team tokens. Net supply reduction.

**Structural read:** The deflationary mechanics are real. This is not a pure emission dump coin. The buyback/burn creates genuine price support tied to product revenue, and the 19% staking yield incentivises holders to reduce circulating float.
