## The Circle Acquisition Story

- Circle acquired Interop Labs (Axelar's dev team) to bring cross-chain tech in-house
- Deal **excludes** the Axelar network, foundation, and AXL token
- Community reaction was brutal — "token vs equity" problem
- Common Prefix (blockchain research firm) took over development
- 2026 roadmap: new protocols, co-staking, institutional features, gasless bridging
