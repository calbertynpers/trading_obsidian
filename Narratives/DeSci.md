# DeSci (Decentralized Science)

**Status:** Heating 🔥🔥
**Strategy:** Long Small Cap
**Last Updated:** Apr 17, 2026

## What It Is

Blockchain-powered scientific research funding. Instead of traditional pharma/VC gatekeeping, communities fund research through DAOs, tokenize intellectual property, and share in the upside of discoveries. The "Y Combinator for biotech on-chain."

## Why It Matters

- Traditional drug development costs $2.6B per approved drug, takes 10-15 years
- 95% of rare diseases have no treatment — no profit incentive for pharma
- DeSci removes the gatekeepers: anyone can fund research, researchers keep IP ownership
- AI + DeSci intersection (BioAgents) is a powerful narrative combo
