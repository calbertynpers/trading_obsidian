# Gaming

**Status:** Cold ❄️
**Strategy:** Watchlist only
**Last Updated:** Apr 17, 2026

## What It Is

Blockchain gaming tokens — play-to-earn, gaming infrastructure, in-game economies. The narrative peaked in 2021-2022 (Axie Infinity era) and has been in decline since. Most gaming tokens are 95-99% below ATH.

## Key Players

| Project | Role | Status |
|---------|------|--------|
| **[[SONIC]]** | Solana L2 for gaming | 97% below ATH. DWF Labs investor. Only 15% circulating — massive dilution. TikTok integration (SonicX). |
| **BIGTIME** | AAA gaming (Big Time Studios) | 98.6% below ATH, $26.6M MCap. Real game with players. FDV 2.62x. |
| **PORTAL** | Gaming token | 99.7% below ATH, $7.3M MCap. Largely dead. |

## Why It's Cold

- Web3 gaming hasn't produced a breakout hit since Axie Infinity
- Most "gaming" tokens are infrastructure plays, not actual games
- The play-to-earn model proved unsustainable
- Traditional gamers are hostile to crypto integration
- Token dilution is extreme across the sector (most 80-90% locked)

## Potential Catalysts

- A genuine Web3 game going viral (hasn't happened yet)
- TikTok/social media integration (SONIC's SonicX approach)
- AI NPCs / AI-generated content in games
- Major studio entering Web3

## Trading Notes

- SONIC has the most interesting angle (TikTok, 2M+ users) but terrible tokenomics (6.67x FDV)
- BIGTIME has the best tokenomics (2.62x FDV) but unclear if the game has traction
- This sector needs a catalyst to trade — don't enter on "it's cheap" alone
- DWF Labs involvement in SONIC is a yellow flag

## Macro Sensitivity

Gaming tokens are moderately sensitive to risk-on macro. They pump in broad altcoin rallies but underperform narrative-driven sectors. The sector needs its own catalyst to truly recover.
