## What It Is

Coins where insiders control 80-90%+ of supply and engineer price action to farm shorts via negative funding and liquidation cascades. Not based on fundamentals — based on manipulation mechanics. The "product" is the squeeze itself.

## Identification Signals

See [[Crime Coin Checklist]] for the full 10-signal framework. Key signals:
- 90%+ supply in few wallets
- Deeply negative funding (shorts paying -1000%+ annualized)
- Uncorrelated with BTC/market
- Not on Hyperliquid
- Primary spot on Bitget
- Inorganic price action (flat → vertical)

## How to Trade

- **Before the climax:** Go long, collect funding (Strategy G)
- **During the climax:** Scale out longs into the spike
- **After the climax:** Flip short, profit-funded stops (Strategy H)
- **Never:** Naked short a crime coin before the climax

## Sub-Categories

### Type 1: Sustained Manipulation (RAVE, MYX, COAI, AIA, M)
- Insiders maintain price at absurd FDV for weeks/months
- Funding deeply negative the entire time
- Strategy G/H applies — ride the manipulation, don't fight it
- The dump comes when insiders decide to exit, not when you think it should

### Type 2: Squeeze-and-Collapse (STO, ORDI, ENJ)
- Short squeeze that reks shorts, then collapses immediately
- The squeeze IS the climax — no sustained manipulation phase
- **This is where FTP shorts work best** — enter after the squeeze collapses
- Timing is everything: too early = squeezed, too late = missed the dump
- STO: 13x pump → full collapse. ORDI: +340% in 2 days, $2.5B volume → likely topped.

### Type 3: Cycling (SIREN, ARIA, PLAY)
- Repeated pump-dump cycles with no clear end
- Each cycle can be traded independently
- Rising floors possible (PLAY pattern)
- Strategy H works well here — long the pump phase, short the dump phase, repeat

## Key Lessons

- Negative funding that kills shorts is income for longs
- The insiders use AI-driven execution — you cannot out-trade them directionally
- The neutral hedge (equal L/S) is the only safe structure against a superior opponent
- Certainty beats potential profit — size for survival, not maximum gain
- When the music stops, those collecting funding are in the path
- "Shorting these coins is a very dangerous game if you don't have a clear framework" — external validation of Strategy G/H
- STO-type squeeze-and-collapse is the exception where FTP shorts work well post-squeeze
