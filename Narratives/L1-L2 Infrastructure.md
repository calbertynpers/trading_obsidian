# L1-L2 Infrastructure

**Status:** Cold ❄️
**Strategy:** Long Small Cap (selective)
**Last Updated:** Apr 17, 2026

## What It Is

Layer 1 blockchains and Layer 2 scaling solutions. The "picks and shovels" of crypto — the infrastructure everything else runs on. Includes modular chains, rollups, app-chains, and zkEVM solutions.

## Why It's Cold

The L1/L2 narrative was the hottest sector in 2021-2024. Dozens of new chains launched, raised billions, and most have failed to attract meaningful usage. The market is oversaturated with infrastructure and undersaturated with users. Token prices reflect this — most are 95-99% below ATH.

## The Problem

Most L1/L2 tokens have:
1. Massive dilution (10-85% of tokens still locked)
2. No differentiation (dozens of "fast, cheap" chains)
3. Declining TVL and usage
4. No catalyst for re-rating

## Exceptions Worth Watching

- **PARTI**: Real revenue ($4.3M), real product (Universal Accounts, 110K users). Chain abstraction is a genuine need. But unlock pressure is brutal.
- **SAGA**: Near ATL with negative funding (longs collect). If modular/app-chain narrative returns, it's the cheapest play. But no catalyst visible.

## Trading Notes

- This is the "wait for catalyst" narrative. Don't enter just because it's cheap.
- SCR is a cautionary tale — TVL can collapse overnight when incentives end
- PARTI has the best fundamentals but worst unlock schedule
- SAGA has the best funding dynamics but no product traction

## Macro Sensitivity

L1/L2 tokens benefit from broad crypto bull markets but lag behind narrative-driven sectors (AI, DeSci, NFTs, memes). They're the "last to pump" in a rotation. In a risk-on environment, capital goes to higher-beta plays first.
