## The Split

The AI narrative has bifurcated:
- **Real AI infra** (TAO, VVV, GRASS, IO, SHELL): actual networks and platforms. These recover when AI narrative heats up.
- **AI-branded meme** (AIA, many others): slapped "AI" on a token with no product. FTP short candidates.
