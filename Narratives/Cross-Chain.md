# Cross-Chain Interoperability

**Status:** Warming 🔥
**Strategy:** Long Small Cap
**Last Updated:** Apr 17, 2026

## What It Is

Protocols that enable communication and asset transfers between different blockchains. As the multi-chain world grows (Ethereum, Solana, Sui, Base, Arbitrum, etc.), the need for seamless cross-chain infrastructure increases.

## Why It's Warming

- Circle acquiring Axelar's dev team validates the cross-chain thesis
- Multi-chain expansion is accelerating (BIO went ETH → Solana → Base)
- Every new chain needs bridges and messaging — structural demand
- AXL's -0.31% funding means longs collect heavily while shorts get farmed

Cross-chain benefits from overall crypto growth — more chains, more users, more need for interoperability. Less sensitive to specific macro events than meme/NFT sectors.
