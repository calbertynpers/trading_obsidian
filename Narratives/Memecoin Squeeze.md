# Memecoin Squeeze

**Status:** Active 🔥🔥
**Strategy:** Observation / Quick scalps only
**Last Updated:** Apr 19, 2026

## What It Is

Periodic squeeze events where beaten-down memecoins pump 2-10x in 1-3 days. Reminiscent of the May 2025 MOODENG 10x squeeze. These are sentiment-driven, not fundamental.

## Pattern

- BSC/Binance memes leading (Binancelife, GIGGLE)
- "Shitty" memecoins squeezing together — sentiment-driven, not coin-specific
- Happens when BTC is constructive and risk appetite is high
- Usually lasts 1-3 days, then fades

## How to Trade

- **NOT for swing positions** — these are 1-3 day events
- Quick scalps only if you catch the move early
- Don't chase after +100% — the squeeze is probably done
- Don't short during the squeeze — wait for exhaustion
- After the squeeze, these typically retrace 50-80% — potential FTP short on the retrace

## Macro Sensitivity

Memecoin squeezes are the purest expression of risk appetite. They happen when BTC is strong, VIX is declining, and CT sentiment is improving. They're a lagging indicator — by the time memes squeeze, the risk-on move is well underway.
