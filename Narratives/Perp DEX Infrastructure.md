## Why HYPE Matters Beyond Price

1. **The Hyperliquid Rule:** >95% of the worst crime coins are NOT listed on Hyperliquid. HL is becoming the "clean" venue — listing on HL is a quality signal.
2. **HLP resilience:** Two attacks in April (XPL and FARTCOIN) resulted in pump-and-dumps on those coins and HLP losses, but HYPE price was unaffected. The protocol absorbs attacks without breaking.
3. **HIP-3 product-market fit:** Oil, precious metals, stocks, indexes on-chain. This is the bridge to tradfi that actually works.
4. **Builder ecosystem:** BASED is built on the HyperLiquid orderbook — the ecosystem is growing.
