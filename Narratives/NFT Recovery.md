# NFT Recovery

**Status:** Early 🔥
**Strategy:** Long Small Cap
**Last Updated:** Apr 17, 2026

## What It Is

NFT marketplaces and infrastructure recovering from a 2+ year bear market. Weekly Ethereum NFT sales hit $12.51M (+70% WoW) in late March 2026. Whale-led recovery focused on blue chip collections (BAYC, Azuki, Pudgy Penguins).

## Why It Matters

- NFTs were the highest-beta sector in the 2021 bull run
- The sector has been dead for 2 years — maximum pessimism = maximum opportunity
- If risk-on macro continues, speculative sectors recover hardest
- NFT infrastructure tokens are 95-99% below ATH — massive upside if sector turns

## Bull Case

- BLUR at $70M MCap with 92% supply unlocked is arguably the cheapest way to play NFT recovery
- Tensor at $13.6M MCap for the dominant Solana NFT marketplace
- Both are market leaders — if NFTs recover at all, these capture the volume
- Risk-on macro (peace deal, rate cuts) is the exact environment where NFTs pump

## Bear Case

- NFTs might not come back. The sector has been declining for 2 years with no sustained recovery.
- Revenue is directly tied to NFT trading volume, which is at multi-year lows
- Blur faces competition from OpenSea (which cut fees to 0%)
- Tensor has 66.5% of tokens still locked — dilution pressure

## Trading Notes

- BLUR has the better tokenomics (92.4% unlocked vs Tensor 33.5%)
- BLUR has the better technical setup (4h golden cross, ADX 57)
- Tensor is cheaper by MCap ($13.6M vs $70M) but more dilution risk
- Both benefit from the same catalyst — NFT volume recovery
- This is a binary bet: either NFTs recover or they don't. Size accordingly.

## Macro Sensitivity

**Highest beta to risk-on macro.** NFTs are pure speculation — they pump hardest in euphoria and dump hardest in fear. Peace deals, rate cuts, institutional buying = NFT fuel. Any risk-off event kills the narrative immediately.
