# Narrative Index

A narrative is the market story driving capital into a sector. Coins pump when their narrative is hot and bleed when it's cold. Identifying which narrative a coin belongs to — and whether that narrative is heating up or cooling down — is as important as the technical setup.

## How to Use This

1. **Before entering a trade:** Check which narrative the coin belongs to. Is the narrative heating or cooling?
2. **Narrative rotation:** When capital rotates out of one narrative into another, the coins in the hot narrative pump regardless of individual fundamentals.
3. **Macro alignment:** Risk-on macro (peace deals, rate cuts, institutional buying) lifts ALL narratives but speculative ones (crime, meme, NFT) pump hardest.
4. **Narrative death:** When a narrative dies (e.g., RESOLV hack killed stablecoin infra confidence), exit immediately regardless of technicals.
5. **Squeeze environment:** Current market produces frequent 1-2 day squeezes. Being flat + opportunistic beats holding passive swing shorts.

## Narrative Heat Scale

- 🔥🔥🔥 **Hot:** Active capital inflows, trending on CT, new entrants
- 🔥🔥 **Heating:** Early signs of rotation, volume picking up
- 🔥 **Warming:** Narrative exists but not yet attracting significant capital
- ❄️ **Cold:** No interest, bleeding, waiting for catalyst
- 💀 **Dead:** Narrative destroyed by event (hack, regulatory, etc.)
