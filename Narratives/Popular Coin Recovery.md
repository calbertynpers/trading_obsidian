**Strategy:** Long if macro bull confirms, watch for higher lows pattern

When popular coins start recovering, it signals broadening risk appetite. Smart money positioned in infrastructure/DeFi first (Apr 17 observation), now the rotation is reaching popular coins. If this continues, it's a strong bull confirmation signal.

## Pattern to Watch

The recovery pattern is:
1. Massive drawdown from launch/peak (70-95%)
2. Extended basing period (weeks to months)
3. Higher lows start forming
4. Catalyst or macro shift triggers acceleration
5. If BTC confirms bull ($80K+), these accelerate hard

## How to Trade

- **NOT FTP candidates** — these have real communities and mindshare
- **Long candidates** if macro bull confirms
- **Entry:** On pullbacks to the higher-low trendline
- **Invalidation:** If higher lows break and coin makes new lows
- **Size:** Moderate — these are recovery plays, not asymmetric bets

## Risk

- If macro turns bearish again, popular coins dump hardest (high beta to sentiment)
- "Popular" doesn't mean "good" — some of these may never recover their peak FDV
- The recovery could be a dead cat bounce in a larger downtrend

## Macro Sensitivity

Extremely high. Popular coins are pure sentiment plays. They need BTC strength and broad risk-on to sustain recovery. If VIX spikes or BTC loses $70K, these dump immediately.
