# Doomed Coin Rotation

**Status:** Early 🔥
**Strategy:** Short-term rotation plays only — not conviction longs
**Last Updated:** Apr 19, 2026

## What It Is

High-FDV tokens that have been bleeding for months with no apparent bottom. The "doomed" label comes from persistent sell pressure (unlocks, lack of narrative, oversupply). But even doomed coins bounce when broad risk appetite returns — and that's happening now.

## Why It Matters

When doomed coins bounce, it's the strongest signal of broad-based risk appetite. If even the worst-performing, most-hated tokens are catching bids, the market is in full risk-on mode. This is a **confirmation signal**, not a trading strategy.

## How to Trade

- **NOT conviction longs** — these are rotation plays
- **Quick in, quick out** — take profit on 30-50% bounces
- **Catalyst matters:** LDO (buyback) and ENA (yield pivot) have real catalysts and may sustain. ARB/OP/WLD are pure rotation and will fade.
- **Size small** — these can reverse violently
- **Stop:** Below the bounce low. If the bounce fails, the doom continues.

## The LDO and ENA Exception

LDO and ENA stand out because they have **structural catalysts** (buyback and yield diversification respectively) that change the fundamental picture. These could transition from "doomed" to "recovering" if the catalysts deliver. Worth monitoring beyond the initial bounce.

## Macro Sensitivity

Extremely high. Doomed coins are the last to bounce in risk-on and the first to dump in risk-off. They are a thermometer for market sentiment, not a portfolio allocation.
