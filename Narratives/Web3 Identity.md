# Web3 Identity

**Status:** Cold ❄️
**Strategy:** Long Small Cap (watchlist only)
**Last Updated:** Apr 17, 2026

## What It Is

Decentralized identity and domain name services for Web3. Instead of wallet addresses (0x7a3b...), users get human-readable names (.bnb, .arb, .eth). Also includes wallet connection infrastructure.

## Key Players

| Project | Role | Status |
|---------|------|--------|
| **[[ID]] (SPACE ID)** | Web3 domain names (.bnb, .arb) | 98.1% below ATH, $14.8M MCap. Real product with users. FDV 4.64x. |
| **WCT (WalletConnect)** | Wallet connection infrastructure | 95.3% below ATH, $11.7M MCap. Used by every dApp. FDV 5.37x. |

## Why It's Cold

- Identity/domains are infrastructure — boring but necessary
- No viral catalyst to drive attention
- Both tokens have significant dilution ahead (60-80% locked)
- The narrative needs a broader Web3 adoption wave to heat up

## Trading Notes

- ID has the better fundamentals (real product, real users)
- WCT is used by literally every dApp but the token has no clear value accrual
- Both are watchlist candidates — wait for a Web3 adoption catalyst
- Neutral funding on both — no cost to hold but no income either

## Macro Sensitivity

Low. Identity/domain tokens don't pump on macro events. They need Web3-specific adoption catalysts (major dApp launches, mainstream onboarding waves).
