# DeFi Revival

**Status:** Warming 🔥
**Strategy:** Long Small Cap
**Last Updated:** Apr 19, 2026

## What It Is

OG DeFi protocols (lending, borrowing, staking, DEXs) that survived the bear market with working products but whose tokens are 95-99% below ATH. The protocols kept building — the market stopped caring. A macro risk-on shift brings attention back.

## Why It Matters

- DeFi TVL at $98B+ and growing
- Aave dominates lending at 60-65% market share, but smaller protocols have real niches
- Liquid staking is the fastest-growing DeFi subsector
- V2 upgrades (Liquity V2, Aave V3) bring new features and potential re-rating

## Bull Case

- LQTY at $30M MCap with zero dilution is the standout. If DeFi lending has any resurgence, Liquity is a proven name with V2 as catalyst.
- HAEDAL's 7.1x TVL/MCap ratio is the best value metric in the entire scan. If Sui grows, HAEDAL captures it.
- DeFi is the "real yield" narrative — protocols that generate actual revenue from actual usage.

## Bear Case

- DeFi is mature and crowded. Aave dominates. Smaller protocols struggle for attention.
- HAEDAL's 80% locked tokens create persistent sell pressure for 2 years.
- DeFi tokens don't pump as hard as meme/narrative coins in bull markets — less sexy.

## Trading Notes

- LQTY is the highest-conviction play: zero dilution, proven protocol, V2 catalyst
- HAEDAL is the best value play: 7.1x TVL/MCap, but dilution is the headwind
- Both have neutral funding — no cost to hold, but no income either
- Entry timing: LQTY hasn't pumped yet (better entry). HAEDAL already bounced 50% from ATL.

## Macro Sensitivity

DeFi benefits from risk-on macro but is less volatile than meme/NFT sectors. It's the "quality" play in a bull market — slower to pump but more sustainable. Rate cuts specifically help DeFi because lower rates make DeFi yields more attractive relative to TradFi.
