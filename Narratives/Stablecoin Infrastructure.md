# Stablecoin Infrastructure

**Status:** Dead 💀
**Strategy:** AVOID
**Last Updated:** Apr 17, 2026

## What It Is

Protocols that build stablecoin infrastructure — issuance, yield, delta-neutral strategies. The thesis was that stablecoins are crypto's killer app and the infrastructure behind them would be valuable.

## What Happened

**RESOLV was hacked on March 22, 2026.** An attacker compromised a private key and minted ~80M unbacked USR tokens, stealing ~$25M. The USR stablecoin crashed 67-95%. The protocol ceased operations.

## Key Players

| Project | Role | Status |
|---------|------|--------|
| **[[RESOLV]]** | Delta-neutral stablecoin (USR) | **DEAD.** Hacked Mar 22, 2026. $25M stolen. Protocol ceased. |

## Lesson

- RESOLV scored well on fundamentals before the hack (low FDV, real product, delta-neutral yield)
- A single compromised key destroyed the entire protocol overnight
- Smart contract / key management risk is existential for stablecoin protocols
- No amount of fundamental analysis protects against a hack

## Trading Notes

- Do not touch RESOLV. The token is a dead asset.
- Be cautious with other stablecoin infrastructure tokens — the hack damaged sector confidence
- If evaluating stablecoin protocols: audit history, key management, and insurance are more important than yield or TVL
