# BDS-005: Cancel Algo/Conditional Orders

**Priority:** High

---

## Summary

Updated `cancel_order` and `cancel_all_orders` to handle algo/conditional orders in addition to standard orders.

## Fix Applied

- **`cancel_order`**: Tries standard endpoint first, falls back to `DELETE /fapi/v1/algoOrder` with `algoId`.
- **`cancel_all_orders`**: After standard cancels, queries `GET /fapi/v1/openAlgoOrders` and cancels each algo order individually.

## Production Verification

Successfully cancelled two old TAOUSDT algo stop orders that the previous `cancel_all_orders` had missed.
