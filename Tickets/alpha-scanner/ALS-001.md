## Summary

Placeholder ticket for a future Alpha Scanner service. The Alpha Scanner is an autonomous process that monitors the market for trading opportunities (Alpha) and registers relevant symbols and trigger conditions into the Price Condition Monitor (BDS-017) for real-time tracking.

---

## Purpose

The Alpha Scanner sits upstream of the Price Condition Monitor. Its job is to:

1. Continuously scan the market (CoinGecko, TradingView, Binance data) for emerging opportunities
2. Identify coins that meet pre-defined Alpha criteria (momentum, volume anomalies, RSI extremes, narrative alignment, etc.)
3. Write discovered symbols to the BDS-017 watchlist table
4. Optionally write associated trigger conditions (e.g. "alert if XYZUSDT drops below $0.05 within 24 hours")
5. Notify via Telegram when Alpha candidates are found

---

## Interface Contract with BDS-017

The Alpha Scanner writes to the shared SQLite database used by BDS-017:

```sql
-- Add a symbol to the watchlist
INSERT OR IGNORE INTO watchlist (symbol, added_by) VALUES ('XYZUSDT', 'alpha_scanner');

-- Optionally register a condition
INSERT INTO conditions (symbol, operator, threshold, action, cooldown_sec, label)
VALUES ('XYZUSDT', 'lt', 0.05, 'agent_notify', 600, 'Alpha entry trigger — XYZUSDT below $0.05');
```

The Price Condition Monitor picks these up on its next refresh cycle with no coordination required.
