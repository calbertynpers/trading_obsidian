## Stop-Placement Math (20x, $90 margin)

At 100k tokens × $0.017997 = $1,800 notional, margin ~$90 at 20x:

| Stop | Distance | Risk | % of margin | Survivability |
|------|----------|------|-------------|---------------|
| $0.01836 | +2% | ~$36 | 40% of margin | Aggressive but defensible |
| $0.01854 | +3% | ~$54 | 60% of margin | Marginal |
| $0.01890 | +5% | ~$90 | **100% of margin (liq zone)** | Do not place stop here — this is the liq |
| $0.01890+ | +5%+ | Liq | — | Leverage says no |

**Critical:** At 20x, the liquidation sits ~5% from entry. That means the stop *must* be tighter than ~4% (~$0.01872) to survive. A **+2% to +3% stop** ($0.01836 to $0.01854) is the realistic band — above that, you're effectively running a no-stop trade hoping the entry is right.

**Trade-off:** The tighter the stop, the more likely a wick takes you out before the thesis plays. 4h RSI 92.7 wicks can easily tag +3% before the real rollover. Two cleaner alternatives (if open to restructuring):
- **Drop leverage to 10x** → stop room doubles, 4% stop survives comfortably
- **Split the 100k into two tranches** → half at $0.017997, half at ~$0.0195 (invalidation above that) → blends basis higher and widens practical stop range
