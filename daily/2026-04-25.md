## Lessons / discipline

### Lesson 1 — Anxiety is a market signal, not an action trigger

The BAS short was working. Funding had peaked deeply negative at the local top, the stop ladder was in place at sensible levels, and the worst-case whipsaw cost was pre-computed at −$13. The anxiety wasn't telling me the trade was wrong — it was telling me **the squeeze was squeezing the late shorts, which usually marks the moment before the move resolves in the original direction.**

Acting on the anxiety converted that diagnostic signal into a $106 realized loss. Had the original Phase 2 plan been left alone, the short TP2 ($0.01150 × 50k) and TP3 ($0.01090 × 25k) would have either fired or stayed armed — both outcomes net positive vs the path I took.

**Pre-commit rule (drafted, not yet enforced):**
> *"I do not modify hedged positions outside of (a) a triggered watchdog alert, (b) a planned TP/stop level being hit, or (c) an explicit reassessment session with written reasoning logged."*

This rule, if respected, would have prevented the BAS unwind today.

### Lesson 2 — Catalyst-pump shorts are a real, repeatable setup

APE pumped 90% on a leadership-change narrative with no immediate cash-flow impact. Volume profile was pure derivatives speculation (+6,460%), not spot accumulation. Whale activity (insider long-then-flip-short) confirmed it was a one-event move.

The mechanical decay pattern I now know to recognise:
1. Day 0: +50-90% news spike
2. Day 1: distribution candle with long upper wick
3. Day 2: dead-cat bounce, funding goes deeply negative as fader-shorts pile in
4. Day 3-5: lower high prints, true markdown to 50-60% retrace of the move
5. Day 5-10: stabilisation at new equilibrium

APE is currently in stage 3. Position is structured to capture stages 4-5 with stops above the dead-cat bounce zone and TPs laddered into prior support.

### Lesson 3 — Recognise the pattern *across* trades

The same setup that played out on NAORIS (Trade 008) — **funding flip as Phase 2 precursor signal** — is playing out on APE right now. Funding hit −0.687% at the local top on April 25 00:00 UTC. That's the Phase 2 precursor for this trade. Same playbook applies: partial close into the signal, hold runner for full mean-reversion target.

This is the second time in five days that "funding direction = signal" has been the right framework. Promote it from "Lessons Bank entry" to **active strategy primitive**.
