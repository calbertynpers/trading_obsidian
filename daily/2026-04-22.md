## 2. Overnight Risk Protocol (New Strategy Doc)

Formalised the CHIP learning into a **6-point checklist** — any limit order being placed into an unsupervised window now gets run through it first.

**The 6 signals:**
1. New listing (< 30 days)
2. Multi-TF RSI > 85
3. Low circulating float (< 25%)
4. Tier-1 VC backing (Hashed, Paradigm, a16z, etc.)
5. Thin orderbook (< $500k on the near side)
6. Trader offline > 4 hours

**≥ 3 signals present = overnight-risky.** Remediation ladder runs from *defer entirely* → *widen stop* → *reduce size* → *schedule cancel* → *scale-in ladder instead of single fill*.

All 6 signals were present on the CHIP short. Had the protocol existed yesterday, the trade would not have been placed overnight.

### Workflow Integration

Before placing any sleep-window limit, the agent now surfaces the checklist and forces an explicit tick-through. Captured in [[Strategies/Overnight Risk Protocol]].
